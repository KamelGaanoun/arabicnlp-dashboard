Update after a new edition

On the Google Scholar venue profile Conference on Arabic Natural Language Processing (ArabicNLP, WANLP), press Show more at the bottom until the list ends.

1. Paper list (recommended)

Tick the box in the list header to select every article, then Export → BibTeX (CSV also works). The export gives full titles and complete author lists, but no citation counts.

2. Citations (optional)

To refresh citation counts, the profile's h-index and its citations per year: save the page with Ctrl/Cmd + S as Webpage, Complete and load the .html file (recommended; "HTML only" misses the articles loaded by Show more). A pasted copy of the page (Ctrl/Cmd + A) also works. If you skip this, citations are carried over from the current data.

Snapshot date

3. Complete author lists (optional)

Scholar exports stop at 10 authors and some Scholar records are incomplete. Load ACL Anthology BibTeX files to fill in the official lists: the volume .bib files (several at once), or the whole anthology.bib.gz. Completed lists are kept in the dashboard, so next time only the new edition's volumes are needed.

4. Acceptance figures (optional)

Main-track figures from the organizers. Copy the cells from Excel (columns Edition, Valid, Accepted, with or without the header) and paste them below, or load a CSV. Editions you paste replace the stored ones; the others are kept.
